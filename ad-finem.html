Mobile App

Ad Finem

Latin for "to the end." A simple lifting notebook for people who train to failure: plan your week once, then log weight and reps for every set.

Concept

Build a workout plan, pick your lift days, and choose the exercises for each day. At the gym, today's workout is already open. Enter the weight and the reps you got to failure, add the next set, done.

Status

Coming soon to the App Store. Free to use, with an optional one-time Ad Finem Pro purchase. No subscription.

Plan

Body area, muscles, exercises, or start from a predefined plan. Add cardio and your own lifts.

Log

Weight and reps to failure for every set, with a warm-up reminder at the start of every session.

Progress

Every lift you repeat gets a chart, so you can see the gains over time.
