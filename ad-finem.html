Mobile App

Ad Finem

Latin for "to the end." A simple lifting notebook built for training to failure, and ready for any workout: plan your week once, then log weight and reps for every set.

Concept

Build a workout plan, pick your lift days, and choose the exercises for each day. At the gym, today's workout is already open. Enter the weight and your reps, add the next set, done.

Status

Available now on the App Store. Free to use, with an optional one-time Ad Finem Pro purchase. No subscription.

Download on the App Store

Plan

Body area, muscles, exercises, or start from a predefined plan. Add cardio and your own lifts.

Log

Weight and reps for every set, or reps alone for bodyweight moves, with a warm-up reminder at the start of every session.

Progress

Every lift you repeat gets a chart, so you can see the gains over time.
