Privacy Policy

Huddle · KB Studio LLC · Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains how the Huddle app ("Huddle," "the app," "we," "us") handles information. Huddle is an iMessage app that helps a group chat pick a place and a day. We built it to collect only what the group needs to see.

What Huddle stores

When you use Huddle in a conversation, the app stores:

  • The name you enter, shown to your group next to your picks.
  • What you add to a huddle: its title and question, places you suggest, days you add, your vote, and the days you mark as free.
  • A random participant ID that Messages gives the app for each conversation. It is not your phone number, email or Apple Account, and Messages does not give Huddle your contacts.

Where it is stored and who can see it

This information is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so everyone in the conversation sees the same huddle. A huddle can be opened by anyone who has its chat bubble, which normally means the people in that conversation. Don't put anything in a huddle you wouldn't share with the group. Apple handles this storage under Apple's privacy policy.

What Huddle does not do

Huddle has no account of its own, no advertising, and no third-party analytics. We do not sell or share your information, and nothing in the app is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Calendar

When you tap Add to my calendar, Huddle opens Apple's standard event screen with the plan filled in. Huddle does not read your calendar. The calendar file the organizer posts is created on their device and sent through Messages.

Deleting your information

You can remove places and days you added while no one else has picked them. To have everything you added to a huddle deleted, email us with the huddle's title and the conversation's approximate date, and we will delete it.

Children's privacy

Huddle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
