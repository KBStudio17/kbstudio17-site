How I work

I start with the wood, not a drawing.

I don’t draw a plan and then go buy lumber for it. I find a piece of wood, look at it for a while, and build around what is already there. Knots, cracks and odd grain stay in. If I sell shirts or other goods, they’re printed only after someone orders, so nothing sits in a box waiting to be thrown away.
