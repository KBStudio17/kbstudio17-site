Work with nature

Don’t Buck Nature.

It’s a reminder I keep for myself as much as anyone. Things last longer when we work with the land than when we fight it.

Order a shirtRead the story

Where it comes from

The phrase goes back to the Dust Bowl.

President Franklin D. Roosevelt pushed for soil conservation during the Dust Bowl. He called for cooperating with nature instead of “trying to buck nature.” That line stuck with me. It’s a little funny and it’s also completely serious.

Healthy land is living land. When the soil stays covered, roots stay in the ground, and grazing and planting are done with care, farms hold up better in hard years. The water runs cleaner too, and there is more left for whoever comes next.

Protect the soil

Disturb it less. Keep living roots in the ground and look after the life underneath.

Work with the land

Natural cycles, careful grazing, a mix of plants and local know-how build land that can take a hit.

Back better practices

Buy from growers and makers who take care of their land and don’t waste what they use.

The shirt

All of the net profit is donated.

Each shirt is printed only after it’s ordered, so there are no boxes of unsold shirts. 100%% of net profits from every Don’t Buck Nature shirt go to organizations working on soil health, regenerative agriculture, conservation and land stewardship.

“Net profits” means the amount remaining after the shirt’s production, fulfillment, payment-processing, taxes, refunds, and other direct order costs. I’ll publish donation recipients and contribution updates on this page.
