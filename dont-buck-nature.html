The Meaning

Nature is not the obstacle. It is the model.

The phrase was inspired by Richard Franklin’s advocacy for soil health and regenerative farming practices. “Don’t Buck Nature” is a witty but serious call to stop working against the systems that sustain us and start rebuilding our relationship with the land.

Healthy land is living land. When soil remains covered, roots stay active, crops and grazing are managed thoughtfully, and biological diversity is protected, farms can become more resilient while supporting cleaner water, healthier ecosystems, and future generations.

Wear the Message

A shirt with a purpose.

Each shirt is produced only after it is ordered, helping avoid excess inventory and unnecessary waste. 100% of net profits from every Don’t Buck Nature shirt sale are donated to organizations advancing soil health, regenerative agriculture, conservation, and responsible land stewardship.

“Net profits” means the amount remaining after the shirt’s production, fulfillment, payment-processing, taxes, refunds, and other direct order costs. Donation recipients and contribution updates will be published on this page for transparency.
